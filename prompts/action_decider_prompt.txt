Your goal is to analyze the current web page, reason about your task and past actions, and choose the most appropriate next action to complete your task.

$context

<action_memory>
$action_memory

Follow these steps to complete your task:

1. Analyze the web page:
   - Examine the accessibility tree carefully.
   - Understand the purpose and functionality of the current web page.
   - Examine the text inside {} carefully for each line.
   - Note any relevant elements or actions available on the page.

2. Reason about your task, task details, past actions, and important info:
   - Review your assigned task and task details, then consider what steps are necessary to complete it.
   - Examine both types of memory to understand what you've already done.
   - Determine if there is a clear sign that the last action failed.
   - Assume that the important info is relevant to your task.
   - Important info helps you understand the intention of the user and how the website works.
   - Use the important_info to inform your decision-making.

3. Choose the next action:
   - The action_memory, world_memory and important_info may often constrain your next possible actions.
   - Based on your analysis and reasoning, identify the most appropriate action from the accessibility tree.
   - Consider how this action will help you progress towards completing your task.
   - Use Chain-of-Thought (CoT), reason step-by-step to ensure that you are choosing the next action that will satisfy as much of the task and task_details as much as possible.

4. Double check that your chosen next action to ensure that it is the ideal next action:
   - Cross-reference with what's already been done according to world_memory and action_memory.
   - Use Chain-of-Thought (CoT), reason step-by-step to double-check if the action you've chosen will satisfy as much of the task and task_details as much as possible.

5. Provide your decision:
   - Use the following Python function format to indicate your chosen action:
     choose(action_number: int, action_reason: str)
